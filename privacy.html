Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026 | Last Updated: September 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Aviko Media ("we", "our", or "the Agency") is dedicated to upholding the privacy, confidentiality, and data integrity of all clients, creative partners, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy clarifies how personal information and project assets are collected, managed, and safeguarded when using our services or submitting project inquiries.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential information required to deliver high-caliber creative services:

  • Direct Communication Data: Full name, professional email address, organization name, and phone contact provided via consultation forms or direct electronic mail.
  • Creative Project Materials: Brand assets, vector logos, brand briefs, reference reels, typography requirements, and project specifications provided for production execution.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized technical log data (such as browser type, operating system, and referral source) used solely for performance monitoring and security defense.

3. Utilization of Information

Client information is processed exclusively for legitimate commercial operations:

  • Preparing formal estimates, creative briefs, and project deliverables.
  • Fulfilling graphic design, branding, and video production obligations.
  • Direct project management communication and milestone reviews.
  • Invoicing, accounting, and legal record compliance.

We do not monetize, sell, lease, or distribute client contact details to third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

4. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

We treat proprietary business models, unreleased product launches, and strategic branding initiatives with the strictest confidentiality. Client materials provided under mutual non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) remain protected and accessible only to assigned studio team members.

5. Data Storage & Security Measures

We implement enterprise-grade security protocols, access-controlled cloud repositories, and encrypted transmission channels (HTTPS/TLS) to prevent unauthorized disclosure, tampering, or loss of project assets and communications.

6. Your Rights & Data Requests

Clients and users may request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of their archived contact details and communication logs at any time, subject to legal compliance and ongoing contractual requirements.

7. Privacy Officer Contact

For privacy-related questions, data removal requests, or confidentiality confirmations, please email our administrative office at avikomedia07@gmail.com.
